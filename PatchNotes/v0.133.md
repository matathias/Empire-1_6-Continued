## 0.133.2 - Variable-length Events
- Date: 2026-03-28
- Type: Patch
- Authors: Matathias

Add the ability to set a variable duration for Empire Events.

### Changes
- Adds timeTillTriggerMax field to FCEventDef. If set, and larger than timeTillTriger, then the event's duration will be randomly chosen from the range (timeTillTrigger, timeTillTriggerMax)

## 0.133.1 - Event updates
- Date: 2026-03-28
- Type: Patch
- Authors: Matathias

Update some events to use the new FCEventDef fields.

### Changes
- Drug Trade has a 30-day cooldown, and can only fire after the Empire faction is 5 days old
- Independence Petitions has a 10-day cooldown, can only fire after the Empire faction is 5 days old, and requires at least 3 settlements in the faction
- Moonlit Ritual has a 30-day cooldown, and can only fire after the Empire faction is 5 days old
- Settling Down has a 15-day cooldown, and requires at least 2 settlements in the faction
- Strange Discovery has a 30-day cooldown, and can only fire after the Empire faction is 5 days old
- Wanderlust has a 15-day cooldown
