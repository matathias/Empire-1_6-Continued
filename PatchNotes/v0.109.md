# Empire Refactored v0.109

## 0.109.0 - Restrict weapon filtering
- Date: 2026-03-18
- Type: Minor
- Authors: Matathias

Adds some restrictions to how weapons are filtered across the mod.

### Changes
- Add "WeaponUnique" to the category blocklist of the weapons resource, thus blocking unique weapons from being tithed
- Block unique weapons from appearing in the weapon selection list for created military units
