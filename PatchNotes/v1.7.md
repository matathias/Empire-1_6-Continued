# Empire Refactored v1.7

## 1.7.0 - RimWorld 1.7 Update
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Type: Major
- Authors: Matathias

Updates Empire for RimWorld 1.7. Finally!

### Changes
- Various backend changes to modernize the code and update file directories
- If the Empire faction hasn't been created, then most of the Empire debug actions are hidden
- Bugfix: in certain niche situations, taking the Road Builders perk could kill the road queue
- Downed mercs that get left behind are eventually just despawned
- Bugfix: mercs that die off-map would remain in their squad slot
- Bugfix: in certain niche situations, launching an Empire shuttles with animals inside would delete all of the animals
- Make the RimWar and World Domination patches somewhat more robust
- Bugfix: tithe cost calcuations were subtly incorrect
- Bugfix: merc deployments counted as a lost battle
- Bugfix: if the player changes the maximum building limit, then settlement building lists could end up mutating during a save or load
- Bugfix: some issues with edict enactment
- If the player has no home map and an Empire battle ends with all of their pawns downed, then the downed pawns are sent to the player's nearest caravan (or form a caravan in place if there isn't one)
- Bugfix: squads could sometimes become available even though they still have pawns on a map
- Bugfix: if a squad refill failed for some reason, then the player would still lose their money
- Bugfix: the battle forecast's odds were off of what the battle simulator actually does
- Events targeting all settlements will skip ones without the intended resource
- Cap animals in settlement trader stock
- Squad deployment check includes squad sub-pawns (e.g. animals, mechs)
- Bugfix: auto battles could unintentionally kill a merc when they're only supposed to be injured
